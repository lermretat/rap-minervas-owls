1. Government readies its anti-terror statute, with terrorist lists that would enable bombings

El Tiempo got the text of the Interior Ministry's statute, to be filed with Congress in the coming days alongside a Justice Ministry surrender ("sometimiento") law. It would create lists of terrorist organizations; groups on them would be classified as organized armed groups under international humanitarian law, which lets the government bomb them. These are two of the administration's flagship bills and the legal core of its hard-line security policy.

2. De la Espriella backs the ex-soldiers accused of killing Haiti's president

It's a sharp break from Petro, who apologized to Haiti, and from his own Foreign Ministry's cautious line in September.
