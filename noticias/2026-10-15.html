1. De la Espriella declares a “cultural counterrevolution” against “wokismo”

From the Culture Ministry, the president said “wokismo” is one of the “great cancers” threatening national unity, vowed an open fight against inclusive language and gender ideology, and announced the ministry will again be called Cultura y Patrimonio, with special protection for religious heritage. It is the sharpest cultural break yet with Petro’s vision of culture.

4. Constitutional Court opens a fiscal-impact case on the pension reform

Unanimously (7–0), the Court gave Hacienda 30 working days to back its claim that starting the reform on April 1, 2027 would leave a roughly COP 5 trillion hole not in the 2027 budget; Colpensiones wants a delay to April 1, 2028. Opening the incident does not yet change the start date.

5. House delays the 2027 budget debate as Hacienda flags COP 4.7 trillion shortfalls

The Cámara postponed second-reading debate to Monday Oct 19, with a hard deadline of Oct 20 before the executive can issue the budget by decree. Minister Miguel Gómez blamed Petro-era gaps of COP 2.7 trillion in Fomag and about COP 2 trillion for older adults, and asked plenaries to drop four first-debate add-ons totaling COP 2.75 trillion.

  • El Tiempo

From an official visit in Israel, Foreign Minister Omar Bula announced withdrawal from the coalition founded under Petro to pursue legal and diplomatic action over Gaza, days after Bogotá opened an embassy in Jerusalem. Bolivia and Honduras have also left.

Both deployments would need congressional approval.
