1. Government files its "acogimiento" bill to make armed groups submit to justice

Interior Minister Rodrigo Lara and Justice Minister Iván Cancino filed the bill with urgent status, stressing it is a legal surrender framework, not a political negotiation: groups must hand over weapons, drug routes and truth, with sentence cuts capped at 30% for leaders and 50% for others, and no share of seized assets returned. It's the core of De la Espriella's security plan, and the anti-terror statute is due next week.
