1. President orders Air Force probe after Eva Ferrer alleges Alcocer used CATAM flights for drugs and cash

After Semana aired ex-presidential adviser Eva Ferrer’s claim that then–first lady Verónica Alcocer headed a network that allegedly moved weapons and drugs to Venezuela on official aircraft and brought cash back, De la Espriella ordered the Air Force commander, Inspectorate and counterintelligence to investigate current and former CATAM personnel. 3. XM warns of possible power shortfall February–April 2027 under El Niño

Grid operator XM said demand may not be fully covered in early 2027 if hydrology stays as weak as 2015–17 and thermal plants fail to hold above about 85 GWh/day on average through April. Reservoirs were at 74.96% on Oct 9—below CREG targets—and Ideam sees a 75% chance this El Niño becomes unprecedented since 1950, with demand already at record highs.
