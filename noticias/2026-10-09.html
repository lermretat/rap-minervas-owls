1. De la Espriella to sign a new decree regulating social protest

Interior Minister Rodrigo Lara said the president would receive the text today, reforming the 2021 protocol so police can act against violent acts without waiting for every dialogue step to finish, while claiming peaceful marches stay protected. The draft defines violent conduct, sets eight grounds to end dialogue (including blocked medical missions and weapons), five response levels, and “solidarity corridors” against permanent road or TransMilenio blockades, days before an opposition march planned for October 14.

2. Ideam confirms El Niño; government races to avert a power crunch

Ideam said Thursday that Pacific temperatures show El Niño is underway, with an 83% chance it stays strong at least through March 2027 and effects lasting into mid-year, meaning below-normal rains especially in the Andean, Caribbean and Pacific regions. Mines and Energy launched a shock plan (savings, fuels and gas, thermal dispatch, existing capacity) aiming to lock in measures by December 31, and local governments have until November 30 to update contingency plans.

3. Urgency message for COP 2.7 trillion quake fund and Air-e energy transfers

The president asked Congress to fast-track a 2026 budget change adding about COP 2.7 trillion to Fondo Milagro for August earthquake reconstruction, lifting the budget above COP 637 trillion if approved, funded mainly by estimated tax-relief collections. The same bill moves COP 1.2 trillion so intervened Caribbean utility Air-e can pay generators—temporary support the finance minister said is meant to protect the grid under El Niño stress, not to “save” the company.

4. President reports 29 arrests in 24 hours and blows to criminal rents
